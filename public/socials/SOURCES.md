# Homepage social logos

Checked against the official brand resources on September 30, 2026.

- `instagram-app.webp`: the filled, 180-pixel app icon published as an Apple touch icon by [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/), downloaded from [Instagram's CDN](https://static.cdninstagram.com/rsrc.php/yw/r/icwX0xAk0pz.webp). Used without modification.
- `linkedin-mark.png`: the filled blue/white icon published by [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/), downloaded from [LinkedIn's CDN](https://static.licdn.com/aero-v1/sc/h/al2o9zrvru7aqj8e1x2rzsrca). The largest embedded ICO image (64 pixels) was losslessly converted to PNG, retaining its original colors and alpha channel.
- `facebook-mark.png`: `Facebook_Logo_Primary.png` from the logo pack linked by [Facebook's Brand Resource Center](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo/).

The Facebook PNG was proportionally resized to 128 pixels. Original colors, artwork, and transparency are preserved. The homepage displays the icons at 40 pixels on phones and 48 pixels from the small breakpoint upward.

`tiktok-logo-clean.svg` is the existing transparent, full-color symbol for dark backgrounds. Its appearance was checked against the logo pack linked by [TikTok's design guidelines](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/getting-started-design-guidelines). It was reused without modification.

The platform logos belong to their respective owners. The older image files remain available for other pages.
